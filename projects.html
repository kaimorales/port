VO2

1st, tartanhacks 2026

An n-of-1 wearable health analysis system. It turns long-term WHOOP, Ōura, and Garmin data into statistically valid personal studies — using changepoint detection, multiple-testing-corrected correlation analysis, and a RAG assistant grounded in peer-reviewed health literature — so people can find what actually moves their own physiology, not the average person's. Won Conway's Best AI for Decision Support Award at Carnegie Mellon.
