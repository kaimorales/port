about

I'm an economics student at the University of Pittsburgh, and I spend most of my time building with AI. I'm interested in systems that help people make better decisions — in health, in sports, and in how organizations use data.

Right now I'm co-founding Harlen, working as an applied AI intern at PPG — scaling a computer vision app and building AI agents for enterprise data — and doing data science at UPMC's MoSHI Lab, where I work on statistical and causal modeling of wearable data in cancer patients.

Before college I spent high school in Tokyo at The American School in Japan, where I founded the aerospace club. I still speak some Japanese. Along the way I've built a college football ranking model, a distributed LLM inference network, and won first at TartanHacks and the Claude AI Hackathon.

The short version: I like taking messy real-world data and turning it into something people can actually act on. If any of that overlaps with what you're working on, reach out.
